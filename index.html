About Pratus

Built in a Gujarati kitchen. Tested in the gym.

Pratus started with a stubborn question: why did eating for performance mean giving up the food that made me who I am?

Every high-protein option was a chicken breast, a shake, or a sad bowl of quinoa. Nothing on the shelf looked like the plates at my parents’ table — rotli and shaak, thepla in a foil pack, paneer paratha on a Sunday.

So we rebuilt them. Same recipes, same hands, re-engineered for the macros athletes actually need. Vegetarian by default. Indian without apology. Culture that performs.
